FOUNDATIONAL CHARTER

Statutory Vision & Purpose

Chanakya University was established under the prestigious Karnataka Act No. 37 of 2021 as a global centre for learning and impactful multidisciplinary inquiry. Named after the timeless polymath Chanakya (Kautilya), the university is founded on the conviction that profound social transformation emerges from the synthesis of traditional epistemology, ethical governance, and technological innovation.

The Research Commons serves as the digital academic spine of the institution. It bridges traditional silos between Biosciences, Engineering, Arts, Management, Natural Sciences, and Law by unifying verified publications, peer review discourse, and vector embeddings into a high-performance open research workspace.
