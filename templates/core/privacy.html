Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 Compliance

Institutional Data Privacy Charter

Effective Date: November 2026 • Chanakya University Research Commons • Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

shield 1. Institutional Commitment & Statutory Governance

Chanakya University ("University", established under Karnataka Act No. 37 of 2021) is dedicated to protecting the privacy, academic freedom, and digital integrity of our faculty, researchers, student fellows, and scholarly partners. This Privacy Charter governs how data is ingested, processed, indexed, and retained within the Chanakya University Research Commons platform.

dataset 2. Categories of Information Processed

A. Academic Profile Credentials (F1)

Name, institutional email, academic rank, school and department affiliation, degrees, research interests, and profile bios.

B. Scholarly Works & Open Access Metadata (F2, F4)

Titles, abstracts, canonical DOIs, arXiv identifiers, publication dates, and author attribution ingested from open scholarly catalogs (Crossref, OpenAlex, arXiv).

C. Vector Embeddings & AI Context (F4, F8)

High-dimensional mathematical vector embeddings (384-dimensional dense vectors) derived from public paper abstracts to facilitate semantic retrieval and RAG question answering.

D. Research Activity Telemetry (F7, F9)

Aggregated search counts, paper bookmarks, and anonymized interaction timestamps used strictly for DuckDB star-schema institutional research analytics.

lock 3. Storage, Encryption & Security Standards

All raw manuscripts and ingestion dumps are stored in secure S3-compliant object stores (MinIO) isolated within university network boundaries. Structured relational data and vector indices are managed inside a dedicated PostgreSQL + pgvector engine enforcing role-based permissions (RBAC). No biometric or personal non-academic information is ever collected or harvested.

verified_user 4. Rights of University Members

Under the DPDP Act 2023, every verified member holds the right to inspect their stored profile, update affiliations, export their curated personal library collections in open formats (JSON/BibTeX), and request profile de-identification upon institutional transition.
