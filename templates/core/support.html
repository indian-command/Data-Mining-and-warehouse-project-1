Subsystem Frequently Asked Questions

F1 – F9

Navigate to your Workspace Dashboard or click Edit Profile. You can configure your academic rank (Faculty, Research Scholar, Student Fellow), choose your official Chanakya University School and Department, update your research bio, and subscribe to university topics (e.g., #Indian Knowledge Systems, #Artificial Intelligence).

The Research Library lets you create custom research folders (e.g. "Genomics Lab Review 2026") and bookmark papers. You can add private analytical notes, assign priority stars, and link external publications directly to university discussion threads.

Every discussion post allows attaching a specific paper from the catalog. Scholars can post threaded replies, upvote high-quality analyses, and filter posts by school or topic tags.

Subsystem F4 ingests records across OpenAlex, arXiv, and curated RSS feeds. Raw JSON payloads are archived in MinIO, while MinHash LSH (Locality Sensitive Hashing) identifies near-duplicate preprints with 92%+ precision to maintain a clean index.

The search bar simultaneously queries 4 domains: (1) Scholarly Sources, (2) Shared Library, (3) University Members, and (4) Discussions. It computes a hybrid score combining PostgreSQL full-text tsvector rank, cosine similarity over 384-dimensional pgvector embeddings, and citation PageRank.

Recommendations blend content-based centroid vectors (calculated from your saved papers and followed topics) with item-to-item co-occurrence and collaborative filtering. Cold-start fallbacks recommend top PageRank papers in your designated department.

Subsystem F7 executes sliding-window statistical tests (7-day vs 30-day baseline). When activity surge reaches Z-score ≥ 2.0, a topic velocity spike is flagged. Automated burst detectors flag accounts exceeding 5 posts per minute for administrator moderation.

The RAG pipeline retrieves the top-k most relevant text chunks via cosine similarity in pgvector. The context is passed to the LLM with strict citation prompting, rendering numbered citations ([1], [2]) directly mapped to clickable source passage cards.

An automated ETL exports activity facts into columnar Apache Parquet files arranged in a star-schema (fact_activity joined with dim_department, dim_user, dim_topic, dim_time). DuckDB executes zero-overhead OLAP aggregations for sub-millisecond drill-down dashboards.
