Terms of Service & Scholarly Honor Code

Governing all academic research, data exchange, discussions, and AI-assisted workflows within Chanakya University Research Commons.

gavel 1. Acceptable Academic Use

The Chanakya University Research Commons is established exclusively to advance academic inquiry, open scientific research, and peer collaboration. Access is granted to verified faculty, enrolled students, research scholars, and authorized institutional affiliates. All automated scrapers, denial-of-service tools, or unauthorized credential sharing are strictly prohibited.

copyright 2. Intellectual Property & Open Access Attribution

Scholarly works indexed within the Commons remain the intellectual property of their original authors, publishers, or preprint servers under their respective Creative Commons or institutional licenses. When citing works discovered through the platform, members must provide full bibliographic attribution including canonical DOIs or arXiv identifiers.

smart_toy 3. AI Research Assistant & RAG Ethics (F8)

The integrated AI Research Assistant synthesizes passages retrieved via cosine similarity over indexed papers. Members agree to the following ethical guidelines:

  • AI-synthesized responses are research aides and must not be submitted as original scholarly work without primary source review.
  • Inline citations provided by the assistant must be cross-verified against primary literature before citing in academic theses or journal submissions.
  • Automated query flooding or jailbreak prompt injections targeting the local LLM runtime are subject to automated moderation flags under Subsystem F7.

forum 4. Peer Review & Discussion Guidelines (F3)

The Research Discussions forum is a civil, evidence-grounded environment. Harassment, abusive language, non-academic promotions, or unsubstantiated claims will be removed by the institutional moderation queue. Subsystem F7 continuously monitors for automated spam bursts or duplicate posting anomalies.

policy 5. Disciplinary Sanctions & Due Process

Violations of this Scholarly Honor Code are reviewed by the Chanakya University Academic Senate and Discipline Committee in accordance with the university statues. Sanctions may range from repository privileges suspension to formal academic review.

Inquiries regarding research copyright or code interpretation may be addressed to: Dean of Research & Academic Affairs • Chanakya University
